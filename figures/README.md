# Figure guide

## Figure 1. Experimental coverage and dependence on the checked source families.

a, VCV records with protein, transport, numerical/censored transport or allele-specific RNA evidence, using the 488-record denominator. RNA is a parallel evidence domain; the bars are not additive. b, Protein coverage within the original all-condition germline classification groups. Direct labels give the numerator/denominator. Coverage records the presence of experiments irrespective of activity direction. P/LP, pathogenic/likely pathogenic; VUS, uncertain significance; B/LB, benign/likely benign. c, Protein-matched VCVs retained after removing each original family, with a dashed line at the 22-record baseline. The leave-one-family-out description measures source dependence of coverage breadth; it does not measure assay validity. Removing a family can preserve a protein match while removing quantitative or condition-specific information. Source data: Supplementary Data S14 and S18; record-level data: S1 and S2.

## Figure 2. Source-verified human variant experiments.

The matrix displays 25 substitutions across six original protein experiment families and one allele-specific RNA source. There are 51 eligible protein result rows in 43 family–substitution cells; 43 transport rows occupy 38 cells. N, numerical summary; C, strict upper bound; R, textual group range; Q, qualitative transport; P, purification/stability; L, protein detection/localization; S, allele-specific splicing. Combined codes retain distinct conditions or endpoints. Codes convey evidence representation, not magnitude. Daggers mark four substitutions outside the strict ClinVar snapshot; double daggers mark engineered-only M477L and P482S matches. The RNA cell specifically concerns NM_014270.5:c.209C>T (A70V). Blank cells contain no qualifying result in this curated matrix. The 25 substitutions map to 22 VCVs representing 21 current substitutions, because V142A maps to two protein-equivalent VCVs. Source clinical observation describes ascertainment and does not establish pathogenicity. Cell-level source data are in Supplementary Data S5, with linked result rows in S2.

## Figure 3. Shared substitutions and conditional phenotypes retain their assay context.

a, Six SLC7A9 substitutions tested by Font (2001) and He (2026). Navy circles show published rounded percentages from Font Table 5; that table does not provide per-variant biological n or dispersion for these values. Teal squares and horizontal error bars show He means ± SD across three independent transfections, each with technical triplicates. Open left-pointing triangles mark strict upper bounds below 5% for V170M and A354T, rather than measured values at 5%. Each study uses its own wild-type normalization. b, Pineda (2004) R365W findings after culture at 33 or 37 °C. The 49 ± 4% summary is mean ± SEM across 11 independent experiments; the other condition is a qualitative no-above-background observation. Uptake measurement temperature is distinct from culture temperature. Studies are displayed separately because system, substrate, concentration and timing differ. Source data: Supplementary Data S16 and the linked condition rows in S2.

## Supplementary Figure S2. Structural distribution of the original contact-region cohort.

Panels a and b show two views of PDB 6LI9 biological assembly 1. C-alpha traces identify chains A/C (rBAT) and B/D (b0,+AT); magenta points show deposited non-polymer arginine ligands B601/D601. Orange markers identify 46 gene-specific residue positions corresponding to the original 54 uncertain/conflicting VCV records at 5 Å. Each site appears in both copies of its subunit; substitutions at the same site share a marker. Source recovery subsequently established protein-matched experiments for V142A and M477L, leaving 52 original candidates without a qualifying result in the checked set. The geometry retains the original cohort. Panel c shows its ascertainment rule at 4, 5 and 6 Å: SLC3A1, 25/28/33; SLC7A9, 18/26/27. Contacts use minimum wild-type heavy-atom distances to resolved ARG, the heterodimer partner or the other heterodimer, across equivalent copies. C-alpha markers show positions only. ARG proximity describes the deposited arginine-bound configuration. The original candidate table is Supplementary Data S4; current threshold-specific membership is in S19. The depicted original 4/5/6 Å counts are 43/54/60; the current unmatched counts after source updates are 42/52/58 and are shown separately in Supplementary Figure S3.

## Supplementary Figure S3. Distance-threshold dependence of current unmatched candidates.

The current rule selects uncertain/conflicting VCV records without a qualifying protein result in the checked six-family set. Bars separate SLC3A1 and SLC7A9; direct labels show gene-specific counts and totals at 4, 5 and 6 Å. Membership is nested across the three thresholds. Distances are minimum wild-type heavy-atom distances to ARG, the heterodimer partner or the other heterodimer in 6LI9 assembly 1. These descriptive counts depend on both source coverage and geometry. Data: S15 and S19.
